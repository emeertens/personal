<h2> Linguistics </h2>
<p>I am interested in formal models of language and meaning and till what extent they can help us understand the interactions of non-human animals, in particular cows. Currently, I conduct ethnographic fieldwork on farms and at petting zoos to see how cows interacht with humans and with eachother. Together with my wonderful colleagues, I explore whether theoretical models of speech-acts and compositionality can be applied to those interactions. 
<br>
<br>
During my PhD, I worked on the question of how meaning arises (semantics) and how we use language to communicate (pragmatics). In particular, I conducted research on question meaning and how this can be derived from prosody. My dissertation is about form-to-meaning mapping in alternative questions and is written within the dfg funded project <a class = "cleanlink" href="https://typo.uni-konstanz.de/questionsInterfaces/">Questions at the Interfaces</a>. I also have work on imperatives and the Macedonian Q-particle <i>li</i>. 
<br>
<br>
At the moment, I am also reflecting on how my work on non-human animals, and particularly dairy cows, can be positioned within ecofeminism.
</p>
