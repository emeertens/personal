        <b>Writing a résumé</b>
        <br> 
        Writing a résumé<br>
        What needs to be done?<br>
        Fill out the application<br>
        and enclose a résumé.<br>
        <br> 
        Regardless of the length of life<br>
        a résumé is best kept short.<br>
         <br> 
        Concise, well-chosen facts are de rigueur.<br>
        Landscapes are replaced by addresses,<br>
        shaky memories give way to unshakable dates.<br>
        <br> 
        Of all your loves mention only the marriage,<br>
        of all your children only those who were born.<br>
        <br> 
        Who knows you counts more than who you know.<br>
        Trips only if taken abroad.<br>
        <br> 
        Memberships in what but without why.<br>
        Honors, but not how they were earned.<br>
        Write as if you’d never talked to yourself<br>
        and always kept yourself at arm’s length.<br>
        <br> 
        Pass over in silence your dogs, cats, birds,<br>
        dusty keepsakes, friends, and dreams.<br>
        <br> 
        Price, not worth,<br>
        and title, not what’s inside.<br>
        His shoe size, not where he’s off to,<br>
        that one you pass yourself off as.<br>
        <br>
        In addition, a photograph with one ear showing.<br>
        What matters is its shape, not what it hears.<br>
        <br> 
        What is there to hear, anyway?<br>
        The clatter of paper shredders.<br>
        <br>
        Wisława Szymborska
